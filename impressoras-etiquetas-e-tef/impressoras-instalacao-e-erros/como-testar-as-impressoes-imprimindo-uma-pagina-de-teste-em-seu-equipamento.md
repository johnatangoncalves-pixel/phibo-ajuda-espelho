# Como testar as impressões imprimindo uma pagina de teste em seu equipamento

1. Abra o PAINEL DE CONTROLE.

Clique no botão iniciar do Windows:

<figure><img src="https://1136259996-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MbXdm46cDzB-VCuUzq9%2Fuploads%2FhYKAM5FLCcwoxDniRSEm%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=cd210b30-36d4-4a73-9781-751739ee6d88" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Depois digite “Painel de controle” e clique em abrir.,

<figure><img src="https://1136259996-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MbXdm46cDzB-VCuUzq9%2Fuploads%2FF03oW21YBgDrcb9N21pk%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=bca076e3-9301-434b-8137-316efdb1fa15" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1\.      Acesse a opção EXIBIR IMPRESSORAS E DISPOSITIVOS.



Selecione a opção de Exibir por: “Categoria” e depois clique em HARDWARE E SONS.

<figure><img src="https://1136259996-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MbXdm46cDzB-VCuUzq9%2Fuploads%2F36pv7gm7aZC0ENcJawmf%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=ccc6a8fc-1454-4e1f-9e63-e327a0509506" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Clique na opção DISPOSITIVOS E IMPRESSORA.

<figure><img src="https://1136259996-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MbXdm46cDzB-VCuUzq9%2Fuploads%2FQSLUgzTQkHXxTWcXhnCz%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=fecbccad-9441-4fa3-ad88-0863b6a16740" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Clique no ícone da sua impressora com o botão direito do mouse e depois selecione a opção PROPRIEDADES DA IMPRESSORA.

<figure><img src="https://1136259996-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MbXdm46cDzB-VCuUzq9%2Fuploads%2FcTlTtJoBcv5FZCGSpOAX%2Fprop%20impressora.jpeg?alt=media&amp;token=30276036-68e5-47d7-9ebd-2342c3ce46b1" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Clique em IMPRIMIR PÁGINA DE TESTE.

<figure><img src="https://1136259996-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MbXdm46cDzB-VCuUzq9%2Fuploads%2FWHQtDG8cScrQ6qcr3yTh%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=d5080620-555f-4aea-b52b-6764119eccae" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
